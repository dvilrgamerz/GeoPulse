## God's Eye View

GeoPulse's v0.3.1 architecture and UX were improved after studying:

- Project: **God's Eye View**
- Author: Bilawal Sidhu
- Repository: https://github.com/bilawalsidhu/gods-eye-view
- Source-code license: MIT

GeoPulse borrows general engineering ideas such as source attribution, viewport-aware network requests, graceful provider fallback, and separating map-stack responsibilities. GeoPulse does not intentionally bundle or redistribute God's Eye View branding, promotional imagery, 3D models, or bundled third-party datasets.

God's Eye View's own LICENSE and DATA_SOURCES documentation state that its MIT grant applies to source code, while third-party datasets/assets retain separate licenses. GeoPulse therefore avoids importing those upstream data bundles by default.

If future GeoPulse commits directly adapt a substantial upstream source-code portion, preserve the applicable MIT copyright and permission notice with that derived code.
